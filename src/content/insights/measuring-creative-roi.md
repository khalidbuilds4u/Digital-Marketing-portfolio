Every CMO has heard this line from their creative agency: "Brand awareness is hard to measure." It's the oldest excuse in advertising, and it's lazy.

Everything is measurable. You just have to know what to measure.

### The Brand Lift Framework

Instead of trying to directly attribute a billboard to a sale, we use a Brand Lift Framework that tracks leading indicators:

- **Direct traffic growth** — Are more people typing your URL directly into their browser? That's brand recall
- **Branded search volume** — Are more people Googling your brand name? Use Google Trends to track this monthly
- **Share of Voice** — What percentage of industry conversations mention your brand vs. competitors?
- **Cost Per Acquisition trajectory** — As brand awareness grows, your CPA on paid channels should decrease over time

### The Creative Scorecard

Every piece of creative we produce gets scored on four dimensions:

1. **Stopping Power** — Does it make someone stop scrolling? (Measured by hook rate / 3-second view rate)
2. **Emotional Resonance** — Does it make someone feel something? (Measured by save rate and share rate)
3. **Message Clarity** — Does the audience understand what we're selling? (Measured by click-through rate)
4. **Conversion Intent** — Does it move someone closer to purchase? (Measured by add-to-cart or lead form fills)

### The Bottom Line

If your agency tells you creative can't be measured, they're either not measuring it or they're afraid of what the numbers would show. Great creative doesn't just look good — it performs. And performance is always measurable.
