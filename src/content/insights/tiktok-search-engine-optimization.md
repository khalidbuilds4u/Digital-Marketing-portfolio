Google isn't the only search engine anymore. According to internal data from Google itself, nearly 40% of Gen Z users turn to TikTok or Instagram when searching for a place to eat, a product to buy, or a service to hire.

This isn't a trend. It's a tectonic shift.

### Why TikTok Search Matters

When a 25-year-old searches "best coffee shop in Dubai" on TikTok, they don't want a list of ten blue links. They want a 30-second video of someone walking in, showing the vibe, tasting the coffee, and reacting in real time. That's infinitely more persuasive than a 5-star Google review from 2019.

### How to Optimize for TikTok Search

The playbook is surprisingly systematic:

- **Keyword-rich captions** — TikTok's algorithm reads your text. Use the exact phrases your audience is searching for in your first line
- **On-screen text** — The algorithm also reads text overlays on your videos. Treat them like H1 tags
- **Hashtags are dead, keywords are king** — Stop using #fyp. Use specific, searchable phrases like "digital marketing agency Dubai"
- **Hook in 0.5 seconds** — Your retention rate in the first second determines whether TikTok shows your video to 500 people or 500,000

### The Agency Advantage

Most brands are still treating TikTok as an entertainment platform. The ones who treat it as a search engine — and produce content specifically designed to rank in TikTok's discovery feed — are quietly stealing market share from competitors who are still obsessing over Google's page one.

The question isn't whether your audience is on TikTok. They are. The question is whether they can find you there.
